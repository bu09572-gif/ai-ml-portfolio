**written program and policies**
- NAIC model bulletin, p. 4: "Consistent therewith, all Insurers authorized to do business in this state are expected to develop, implement, and maintain a written program (an “AIS Program”) for the responsible use of AI Systems that make, or support decisions related to regulated insurance practices."
- NAIC model bulletin, p. 4: "The AIS Program should be designed to mitigate the risk of Adverse Consumer Outcomes, including, at a minimum, the statutory provisions set forth in Section 1 of this bulletin."

**inventory and documentation**
- NAIC model bulletin, p. 6: "Documentation requirements should be developed with Section 4 in mind."
- NAIC model bulletin, p. 7: "3.0 Risk Management and Internal Controls The AIS Program should document the Insurer’s risk identification, mitigation, and management framework and internal controls for AI Systems generally and at each stage of the AI System life cycle."

**data quality and sources**
- New York CL 7 (2024), p. 1: "Therefore, insurers should take an approach to developing and managing their use of ECDIS and AIS that is reasonable and appropriate to each insurer’s business model and the overall complexity and materiality of the risks inherent in using ECDIS and AIS."
- New York CL 7 (2024), p. 1: "This Circular Letter is not intended to provide an exhaustive list of potential issues that could arise from the use of ECDIS or AIS and is not intended to suggest that an insurer’s due diligence in assessing ECDIS or AIS should be limited to the concerns enumerated below."
